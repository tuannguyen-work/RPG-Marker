# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

This app is a player for games you import yourself. It is designed to collect nothing.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send
your games, saves or any other data to us or to third parties.

## Data stored on your device

- **Games you import** are copied into the app's storage on your device.
- **Saves, play time and settings** are stored on your device.
- **iCloud Saves** (optional, on by default): if you are signed in to iCloud, your save files are
  stored in the app's private iCloud Drive container so they sync between your devices. This data
  is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We cannot
  access it. You can turn this off in the app's Settings, and remove the data in the iOS Settings
  app under your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

## Links

Links to the source code or to report a problem open github.com in your browser, which has its
own privacy policy.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published in this file, along with the app's source code.
