RPG Deck turns your iPhone into a handheld console for the PC role-playing games you already own.

Import a game from the Files app and play it full screen in landscape, with on-screen buttons placed right under your thumbs.

SUPPORTED GAMES
Windows games made with RPG Maker XP, VX, VX Ace, MV and MZ. The engine is detected automatically when you import.

YOUR LIBRARY
• Import ZIP files or folders from Files, iCloud Drive or the Share sheet
• Covers made from each game's own title screen
• Search, favorites, filters and sorting
• Play time and "Continue" for the game you played last

MADE FOR TOUCH
• Direction pad with diagonals, A, B, Menu and Turbo/Dash buttons
• Adjustable button size and opacity, optional vibration
• Pause any time to change speed, hide the buttons or quit
• Tap-to-move in MV and MZ games

SAVES THAT FOLLOW YOU
• Saves sync with iCloud to your other devices
• Deleting a game keeps its saves in iCloud; import it again and they're back
• Export and import saves as ZIP files

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
An illustrated guide walks you through adding your first game, the controls, saves, and fixes for common problems.

PRIVATE AND OPEN
