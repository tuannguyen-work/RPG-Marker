RPG Deck is a player for role-playing games the user already owns. It does not include, sell or download any games, and it has no accounts or in-app purchases.

To try it without any other game, the app includes a short demo game made for it:

1. Open RPG Deck. On the empty library, tap "Play the Demo" (it is also in the Guide, the ? button).
2. The demo is added to the library and starts in landscape with on-screen buttons. Press A on "New Game".
3. Walk with the direction pad, press A to talk to the old man, open the chest, or save at the golden crystal. The pause button is at the top left.

The demo's engine is the RPG Maker MV corescript (MIT License); its graphics, sounds and data were made for this app (CC0). It uses no RPG Maker RTP assets. Licenses: Settings → Acknowledgements.

To test importing, any ZIP of the demo or of a Windows RPG Maker XP–MZ game can be imported with the + button. The in-app Guide shows the steps with illustrations.

Saves sync through the app's iCloud Drive container when the device is signed in to iCloud; this can be turned off in Settings.

Game code runs only inside the app: MV/MZ games (HTML5/JavaScript) in WKWebView, XP/VX/VX Ace games in the bundled open-source mkxp-z runtime (Ruby). The app itself is open source under GPL-3.0-or-later.
